# Review and launch checks

- Booking buttons open a clearly labelled placeholder. They do not create appointments.
- Enquiry forms demonstrate validation only and never send messages.
- Preview pages have noindex/nofollow metadata and a disallow-all robots.txt.
- Original live domains and DNS are untouched.
- All downloaded imagery is included as optimised local assets; untouched originals are saved in Original-website-backups.zip.
- archive/ contains complete page text, compressed source HTML, source URLs and image provenance.
- page-index.csv maps discovered existing pages to mockup pages; no redirects are active.
- Re-check sitemap discovery, unlinked pages and any failed downloads before treating the inventory as exhaustive.
- Client approval is needed for final copy, imagery, credentials, prices, timetable, policies and image permissions.
- Before launch: connect bookings and forms, update privacy wording to match actual providers, review tracking/cookie requirements, enable indexing, confirm metadata and put a tested redirect plan in place.
- Terms and safeguarding PDFs have been retained locally. The homepage has a Privacy Policy label with no working link; obtain the full policy before launch.
- Existing training-day dates are from August 2025; these are archived and not promoted as upcoming.
- Weekly timetable is carried over for visual review and needs confirmation.
